ABOUT RGCSM

Rajeev Gandhi Computer Saksharta Mission (RGCSM) is a leading computer education and skill development organization in India, registered under the Societies Registration Act and affiliated with multiple government bodies.

We are ISO 9001:2015 certified and recognized by NSDC, MSME, and partnered with prestigious organizations for delivering quality technical and vocational education across the nation.
