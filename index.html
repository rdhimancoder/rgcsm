About Our District

A Legacy of Excellence in Education

Serving the vibrant communities of Rio Grande City and Sharyland with dedicated, student-centered education.

Rio Grande City Sharyland Municipal School District is a thriving public school system located in Starr County, Texas. We proudly serve thousands of students across our campuses with a focus on academic rigor, character development, and preparation for college and career success.

Programs & Services

We offer a wide range of programs designed to meet the diverse needs, interests, and goals of every student.

STEM Education

Cutting-edge science, technology, engineering, and math programs with state-of-the-art labs.

Fine Arts

Band, choir, theater, and visual arts programs that nurture creativity and artistic expression.

Athletics

Competitive sports programs that build teamwork, discipline, and physical fitness in students.

Bilingual Education

Comprehensive bilingual and ESL programs supporting English-language learners at all levels.

Career & Tech Ed

Industry-aligned CTE programs preparing students for high-demand career pathways.

Special Education

Individualized support and inclusive environments for students with diverse learning needs.

Dual Enrollment

Earn college credits while still in high school through our partnerships with local colleges.

Counseling Services

Dedicated counselors supporting students' academic, social, and emotional well-being.
